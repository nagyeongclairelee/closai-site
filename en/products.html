What holds for every model

Delivered as a finished product

Not a subscription. We build it and hand it over, so there's no monthly fee, and your data and accounts stay yours.

You pay AI and server costs directly

Around ₩30,000–80,000 a month, varying with usage. Paid from your own accounts, not through us. We can't control price changes by external services.

Free support for 1 month, then per item

For one month after delivery we fix bugs (anything not working as specified) for free. After that, check-ins, added features and new integrations are proposed per item.

Irreversible actions get human approval. Sending, payments and deletion never run automatically. They go through the approval inbox.
