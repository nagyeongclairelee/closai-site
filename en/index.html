In a single in-person meeting we write down your repetitive work across 17 items. What can be automated, where a person must approve, what needs connecting: these come from rules, not gut feeling. Different staff, same conclusion.

02

Something real before any pitch

Before any contract, we build a sample dashboard that runs on your actual data. Not a mockup. You see it work, then decide on the full build. No other option lets you see the real thing before deciding.

03

AI that moves only within a set order

The AI agent doesn't judge on its own. It works only within the workflow and approval steps we agree on with you, and anything irreversible (sending, payments, deletion) always gets a human approval. It's the design approach OpenAI and Anthropic recommend.

Daily repetitive work such as sorting inquiries, the booking timeline and settlement totals gets processed in a set order, and you're called in only when an approval is needed. Your data and accounts stay yours, and the screen keeps running even after our relationship ends.
